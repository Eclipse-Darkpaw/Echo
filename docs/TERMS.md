# Echo Web Admin Terms of Service

These terms apply when you authorize the "Echo Web Admin" Discord
application and use the administrative website behind it.

## What the panel is

The panel is volunteer-run tooling for the Echo Discord bots. It lets
server staff configure and manage the bot features of the servers they
are responsible for. It is not a commercial service and carries no
service level guarantees.

## Conditions of use

* Use the panel only for servers where you are an administrator, or where
  a server admin has explicitly granted you access.
* Configuration changes you make take effect on the running bot
  immediately and are recorded in an audit log with your Discord user.
* Content you submit through the panel (prompts, yap messages, team and
  score data) is stored by the bot and displayed in that server.

## Acceptable use

Do not attempt to bypass the access controls, disrupt the panel or the
bots behind it, or use the panel to break the rules of the servers it
manages. Access can be revoked at any time by the server staff or the
bot maintainers.
